Charting minutes played in European finals by nationality, from 1956 to 2025, turns
a familiar story into something you can see happening.

The early decades belong to a handful of countries. Italian, Spanish, German and
English players account for most of the pitch time, and the peaks are extreme:
**15 Italian players** in the 2000 final, **15 Spanish players** in 2003.

Then the bands narrow and multiply. The modern era spreads minutes across a far
wider set of nationalities, and the change accelerates after a specific moment —
the **1995 Bosman ruling**, which removed limits on foreign players in European
competition.

A stacked area chart is the right form here because the question is about
composition over time rather than any single value. What matters is the changing
share of the whole, and how many distinct bands it takes to fill it.
