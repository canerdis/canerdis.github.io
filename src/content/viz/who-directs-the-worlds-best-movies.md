The IMDb Top 250 usually reads as a Hollywood list. Sort it by where each director was born rather than where the money came from, and it turns out to be far less American than it looks.

Every square in the grid is one film, ranked from #1 at the top left to #250 at the bottom right, coloured by the director's birthplace.

| Director born in | Films |
|---|---|
| United States | 110 |
| Europe | 84 |
| Other regions | 51 |
| Co-directors from different regions | 5 |

## Hollywood is the address, not the origin

137 of the films are American productions, but only 92 were both made and directed by Americans. The other 45 were directed by someone born abroad, 35 of them in Europe.

The traffic runs one way. Of Europe's 84 films, 35 were American productions; only 15 European productions had an American director. Europeans go to Hollywood; Americans mostly stay home.

## A few names carry the European share

Christopher Nolan, born in London, has nine films on the list, more than any other director. Hitchcock, Wilder and Chaplin have five each, and Sergio Leone four; of those, only Leone made his films in Europe. Read the European names in order and a second pattern appears: many of them emigrated. Some were chasing bigger budgets. Several, like Wilder after 1933, were running for their lives.

## Caveats

Birthplace is not nationality: a director born in one country may build a whole career in another. That is the point of the chart, but it is still a choice. The list itself is voted by IMDb users, so it reflects that audience's taste rather than an objective ranking of world cinema. Snapshot taken on 6 August 2026.
